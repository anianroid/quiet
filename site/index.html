What Kamui does

In Naruto, Kamui pulls things into a dimension nobody else can see. Same energy: a fly on the wall that is only a fly on the wall.

01 · SEES

Notices the meeting

Zoom, Meet, or Teams, native or in the browser. It watches for a live call window on your screen. No calendar access, no bots joining.

02 · SILENCES

Clears the room

Notetaker banners, “Start Notetaker” pills, and pop-ups vanish the frame they appear, from any app, even ones we've never heard of. Your apps stay usable; only their meeting spam disappears.

03 · WRITES

Takes the notes

Apple speech and Apple Intelligence, on device. When the call ends: Keep or Discard. Junk calls never even ask.
